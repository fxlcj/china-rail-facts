# Known gaps and deliberate exclusions

This file is **not a dataset**. Nothing here is a verified fact and nothing here may be quoted as one. It records the figures we wanted to include but could not verify against an official source, together with entries we refused because the evidence did not hold. It exists so that a later maintainer does not quietly fill these holes with a guess.

| Missing or excluded | Why it is not in `facts.json` | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| How a child's 14th birthday is treated on the day itself | China Railway's English FAQ states the 6- and 13-year thresholds but is silent on the exact treatment of the birthday. The guide says so rather than guessing a number. | Pillar article drafted 2026-11-11; see the site's travel hub. |
| A single figure for station-by-station security screening practice | We could not verify an official page that states one nationwide figure for how individual stations apply the rules. The guide points to the detailed article instead of publishing a number. | Pillar article drafted 2026-11-11. |
| Internal links, affiliate links and sentence fragments picked up by the first automated sync | All 22 candidates from that run were reverted on 2026-11-10. Only complete sentences linked to an official source deep link are eligible for this repo. | `CHANGELOG.md`, 2026-11-10 quality revert. |

If an official page becomes available, add the entry through the monthly maintenance task with its official source URL and check date, record the change in `CHANGELOG.md`, and remove the row above. The official page, not this file and not a third-party mirror, is always the authority.
